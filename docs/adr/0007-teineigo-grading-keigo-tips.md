# ADR-0007: Teineigo is the grading bar; keigo is tips, never a failure

For Call Review, the learner is held to **teineigo** (polite です/ます forms): failing to use
teineigo triggers strong feedback. **Keigo** (honorific/humble forms) is offered only as
optional tips and is never treated as a failure. Review output is per-turn correction plus an
overall assessment, all explanations in English.

Rationale: the MVP teaches practical phone-call Japanese. Holding learners to keigo would
overreach for the target level and turn practice into discouragement; requiring teineigo keeps
the call usable and correct without demanding mastery of honorifics. Explanations are English
because the learner is an English speaker.

Open items (measure in S0, not fixed here): the no-match escalation (repeat → hint → help) and
the English-handling quip (ソーリー、ノー・イングリッシュ) are behavioral/content-volume details
tracked in PLAN.md §5.3, not irreversible decisions.
